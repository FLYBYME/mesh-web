Add `needs('lifecycle')`: page-level events a part subscribes to, with the

The four the issue names, and no more this dispatch:

THE DECISION THAT IS THE REAL DELIVERABLE

**Does the kernel filter the error stream per part?**

Handing every part every unhandled error on the page is a real disclosure —
one part's stack traces reaching another, on a page where parts come from
different publishers. Narrowing to "your own errors" may not be determinable
at all: a `window.onerror` gives you a message, a source URL and a stack, and
mapping that back to which part's code threw is guesswork unless the artifact
boundary happens to be visible in the URL.

Argue it in your plan comment BEFORE writing code, and write the conclusion in
the capability's own doc comment — not the PR, the code. Whichever way you go:

  * if you filter, say how you attribute an error to a part, and what happens
    to errors you cannot attribute
  * if you do not filter, say plainly in the comment that any part with
    `lifecycle` sees every part's errors, so the next person granting it knows
    what they are granting

A comment that says "we chose not to filter, here is the disclosure that
implies" is a complete answer. Silence is not.

THE KERNEL OWNS THE LISTENERS

That is the point of the capability, not a detail. `telem` currently does
`addEventListener` by hand, guards every access with
`typeof window !== 'undefined'`, and is responsible for its own removal. After
this, the kernel adds and removes them the way it does for every other
capability, and disposal is not a part's problem.

WHAT THIS IS NOT

Not a driver. There are not two competing implementations of the page's error
events; there is one browser. Do not build a registry, a selector, or a record
that chooses one. If you find yourself writing the word "provider", stop.

(The driver shape does exist one level down and `telem` already found it:
`transport.ts` takes an injectable sender so `sendBeacon` and `fetch` are
swappable. Leave it alone — it is right, and it is not this issue.)
